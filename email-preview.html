We’re joining Mozilla Foundation’s Human-Scale AI Incubator

Hi there,

Tiles Privacy has joined Mozilla Foundation’s Human-Scale AI Incubator as one of three anchor projects.

I started Tiles with a simple belief: as AI assistants access more of our lives, the risk of data leaks will make privacy mainstream, and people will demand control of their data and identity.

Mozilla’s support gives us 18 months of runway to build that future without giving up equity or control. We’re focusing on private, user-owned AI and humane software, without ads or addictive mechanics.

We’ll work with nonprofits and educational institutions on pilots serving charitable and public-interest goals, and open-source a governance and safeguarding framework that other organizations can adapt.

Thank you to Mozilla Foundation, our sponsors, advisors, and everyone in the User & Agents community who helped us reach this milestone.
