# Licenses

Tiles is free to use without limits. Tiles Pro is the one optional license, and it funds independent development.

## Overview

- **Free**: every client side feature that runs locally, plus every collaboration feature, including shared chats on the web.
- **Tiles Pro**: $10 USD per user, per month, covering the private services we host on your behalf.
- **Commercial use**: covered by Tiles Pro. Tiles itself remains free to use without payment.

## Tiles Pro
