# Licenses

Tiles is free to use without limits. Tiles Plus and Tiles Pro are the optional licenses, and they fund independent development.

## Overview

- **Free**: limited private web searches, every client side feature that runs locally, and shared chats on the web.
- **Tiles Plus**: $3 USD per user, per month, covering peer to peer sync and collaboration features.
- **Tiles Pro**: $10 USD per user, per month, covering everything in Tiles Plus along with the private services we host on your behalf.
- **Commercial use**: covered on every tier, including Free. No license purchase is required to use Tiles at work.

### Your license key

Tiles Pro is delivered as a **license key**, issued automatically by Polar at checkout and revoked when a subscription ends. There is no account to create.

- Find your key in the receipt email or the [customer portal](https://polar.sh/tilesprivacy/portal/).
- Each key activates on a limited number of devices, and you can deactivate a device to free up a slot.
- Key activation inside Tiles ships with the 0.5.0 Private Beta.
